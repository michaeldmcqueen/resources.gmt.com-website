## **Planning Timeline**

* **When you register:** pay your deposit (by October 31 if you registered June through October).
* **Fall to winter:** [recruit your team](/community-service/1-getting-started/recruit-your-team/) and hold the [First Pre-trip Meeting](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/first-pre-trip-meeting/).
* **March 1:** half of your remaining balance is due.
* **Early spring:** hold the [Pre-trip Meeting/Event](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/pre-trip-meetingevent/) (crew practice) and the [Adult Pre-trip Meeting](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/adult-pre-trip-meeting/).
* **Mid-spring:** hold the [Touch Base Pre-trip Meeting](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/touch-base-pre-trip-meeting/).
* **May 1:** your final balance is due.
* **About 6 weeks before your trip:** hold the [Final Pre-trip Meeting](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/final-pre-trip-meeting/).
* **30 days before your trip:** finish the checklist below.
* **The Sunday before you leave:** hold the [Commissioning Service](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/commissioning-service/).

## **General**

* I have my **customer account username and password** and have logged in to review all trip details.
* I have shared the **“Link to Participant Form”** with every youth and adult participant.
* I have **at least one adult (21+) for every five participants under 21**.
* If my group includes **both male and female participants**, I have **at least one male and one female adult** registered.
* Every participant is at least 11 years old or entering 6th grade in the fall (at least 14 or entering 9th grade for Senior High trips).
* I have made all **payments** on time: the deposit, the March 1 installment, and the final balance. All payments are due and non-refundable on **May 1, 2027**.

---

### **Participant Sign-Up**

* Under **Participant Info**, every **participant age 17 or younger** has had a **parent/guardian** complete and digitally sign their form.
* Under **Participant Info**, every **participant age 18 or older** has filled out and digitally signed their own form.
* I have **verified each participant’s entry**—checking for:
  * Duplicates (deleted any extras)
  * Correct spelling
  * Accurate age and gender

---

### **Screening & Verification**

* Every **participant age 18 or older** (including students) has completed a **multi-state/national background check** within the last **2 years**. See [Screening Requirements](/community-service/1-getting-started/screening-requirements/) for the full rules.
* All background checks have been **reviewed by the pastor** and are **securely on file**.
* In my **customer account**, under **Screening & Verification**:
  * I entered each 18+ participant and their background check details.
  * I clicked **“Send Authorization Message”** so my pastor can **digitally authorize** each adult.

---

### **Vehicles & Drivers**

* I verified that all **vehicle, driver, and insurance information** is current and accurate.
* Each vehicle can **transport at least 6 people**.
* My vehicles and drivers are **available daily** to transport crews during camp.

---

### **Special Requests**

* I entered any **special requests or unique situations** in the **“Special Requests”** section of my account, including food, lodging, and crew assignment accommodations.
* I understand GMT will review, approve, or ask for clarification as needed—but **special accommodations are not guaranteed**.
