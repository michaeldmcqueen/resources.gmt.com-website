We suggest you have this meeting in early winter, once you know which participants are attending.

Parents want to know their child will be safe, cared for, and growing in their faith. This meeting answers their questions and shows them how they can help.

### **Before the Meeting**

* Log into your Group Mission Trips customer account and have it open during the meeting.
* Print a [Parent Letter](/community-service/5-helpful-resources/parent-letter/), the [Schedule](/community-service/2-schedule-menu-packing/schedule/), and the [Packing List](/community-service/2-schedule-menu-packing/packing-list/) for each family.
* Be ready to show the Week of Hope Promo Video (on the [Videos](/community-service/5-helpful-resources/videos/) page under Helpful Resources). Test the link ahead of time.
* Have your trip dates, your total cost per participant, and your church's payment schedule ready.
* Invite the adult leaders who are going on the trip, so parents can meet them.
* **Optional:** Ask a parent whose child went on a past mission trip to share what their child was like when they came home.

### **Welcome**

**Say:** Thank you for coming! This summer our youth group is going to **(your mission trip location)** during **(your dates)** with Group Mission Trips. Tonight is for you: what the week looks like, how your child will be kept safe, what it costs, and how you can help.

**(Play the Week of Hope Promo Video, then introduce each adult leader.)**

### **What the Week Looks Like**

**Say:** Your child will be on a crew of six people, one or two adults and four or five youth, usually each from a different church. Crews usually serve with the same local ministry or nonprofit all week. That could be a day camp for kids, a food bank, a Vacation Bible School, or helping older adults.

**Say:** Every morning and evening there is a program focused on Jesus, and our youth group meets every night for devotions.

* Walk through the Schedule so parents can see a typical day.
* Mention Tuesday evening free time and what our group plans to do.

### **Keeping Your Child Safe**

**Say:** Safety is Group Mission Trips' top priority. Here's how our group and GMT take care of your child:

* There is 1 adult (21 or older) for every 5 youth. If both boys and girls are going, at least one male adult and one female adult go too.
* Every participant 18 or older has a national background check, no older than two years, and our pastor signs off on each one. See [Screening Requirements](/community-service/1-getting-started/screening-requirements/) for the full rules.
* Every GMT staff member has been screened, background-checked, and reference checked.
* Crews stay together at all times. No one is ever left alone.
* Showers have separate times for those 17 and younger and those 18 and older.
* Every crew has a first aid kit, the GMT office phone number, and directions to the closest hospital.
* No one is required to do any work they consider unsafe.

### **Health, Medications, and Food**

* Youth leaders keep and give out their own students' medications during the week. GMT staff do not hold or give medications.
* Health insurance is not required, but it is highly recommended. Please give us a photocopy of your child's insurance card.
* If your child eats gluten-free or has food allergies, tell us now so we can let GMT know. Peanut butter is on the menu, and participants with special diets may bring their own food.
* Hand out any medical or permission forms your church uses.

### **Staying in Touch**

**Say:** Closer to the trip, we'll give you the lodging address and how to send mail to your child during camp. Contact information for camp staff will be in our customer account, under the lodging information, in case there's an emergency.

### **Cost and Payments**

* Share the total cost per participant and what it includes (transportation, registration, and free time).
* Share our church's payment dates. They will be ahead of GMT's deadlines of March 1 and May 1.
* Explain that cancellations after May 1 are non-refundable.
* Share our fundraising plans. The [Support Letter](/community-service/5-helpful-resources/support-letter/) and the [Missions Fundraising E-book](/community-service/5-helpful-resources/missions-fundraising-e-book/) can help.

### **Forms**

* Show the [Code of Conduct](/community-service/1-getting-started/code-of-conduct/) and the [Liability & Release Agreement](/community-service/1-getting-started/liability-and-release-agreement/).
* Explain the Participant Info Form. For participants 17 and under, a parent or guardian completes and signs it. Signing it means agreeing to the Code of Conduct and the release.
* All forms must be finished at least 30 days before the trip.

### **What to Pack**

Hand out the Packing List. Point out the twin-size air mattress and bedding, modest clothing, and sturdy closed-toe shoes.

### **How Parents Can Help**

* Pray for our team, and for the people we will serve.
* Help with fundraising and with our Community Gift.
* Come to our [Commissioning Service](/community-service/3-pretrip-meetings/commissioning-service/) the Sunday before we leave.
* Encourage your child to do the Pre-trip Devotions.

### **Questions**

Field any questions (use the [FAQs](/community-service/5-helpful-resources/frequently-asked-questions/) as needed) and close in prayer:

"God, thank you for these families and for the students they are sending to serve you. Keep our team safe, and help us show your love to everyone we meet. In Jesus' name, amen."

### **After the Meeting**

* Send the Parent Letter and the meeting details to any family who couldn't come.
* Send the Participant Info Form link to every family.
