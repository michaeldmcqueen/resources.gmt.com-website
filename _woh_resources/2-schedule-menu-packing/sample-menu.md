(All meals are subject to change based on location and availability.)

At Group Mission Trips, meals are prepared by one of our summer staff members and you! Your youth group will be assigned one or more meal shifts, and your participants will help prepare meals for the rest of camp. It’s good, hearty food—served cafeteria-style! While it may not be restaurant dining, we promise it will keep your crew energized for a full day of serving.

### **Breakfast**

Start the day with a satisfying breakfast! Participants will enjoy one or more of the following options:

\- Main items: French toast, sausage, English muffins, eggs, cheese, biscuits and gravy, or bacon

\- Daily accompaniments: Cereal, yogurt, toast, peanut butter, fruit, oatmeal, milk, coffee, and juice

\- Please note: Friday morning will be a continental breakfast.

### **Lunch**&nbsp;

Lunch is picnic-style at the project sites. Each day includes:

· A variety of lunchmeat sandwiches

· Chips

· Fruit or vegetable

· A treat (like a granola bar or cookie)

· Peanut butter and jelly available daily

Project Site Beverages:

· Each participant should fill their personal water bottle before leaving the facility.

· Every crew will bring a 5-gallon water container to refill bottles on-site.

### **Dinner**&nbsp;

Here’s a sample of what the week may look like:

**Sunday**: Pizza&nbsp;

**Monday**: Pulled pork, mac and cheese, vegetables, and dessert

**Tuesday**: Dinner on your own&nbsp;

**Wednesday**: Taco bar with flour tortillas, taco shells, tortilla chips, ground beef, black beans, lettuce, tomato, cheese, onion, sour cream, salsa, and dessert

**Thursday**: Pasta, marinara and alfredo sauce, vegetables, dinner rolls, and dessert

**Salad bar available every night**: Featuring mixed greens, carrots, hard-boiled eggs, black beans, croutons, sliced turkey or ham, and Ranch and Italian dressing

**Beverages:** Lemonade will be available&nbsp;

Dessert items may include: Ice cream cups, ice cream sandwiches, popsicles, or strawberry shortcake

### Dietary Restrictions & Food Allergies

Have a food allergy or special diet? Put it in the Special Requests section of your customer account before camp. See [Dietary Restriction Accommodations](/community-service/2-schedule-menu-packing/dietary-restriction-accommodations/) for what we can and can’t accommodate.

Signs in the cafeteria will list the vegetarian options each day, and you can see them in the list below.
