## Theme: Unfiltered

Throughout the week, we will explore what it means to live an Unfiltered life with God. Being Unfiltered is about coming before God exactly as we are, without pretending or hiding our questions, doubts, struggles, or failures. Through David’s prayer in Psalm 139, we will discover what it means to invite God to search our hearts, know us completely, and see us as we truly are. We will learn to trust in his grace and allow him to lead us as we continue living an Unfiltered life with God beyond camp.

**Theme Verse: “Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. See if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me along the way everlasting.” – Psalm 139:23–24**

### SUNDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered: Come as You Really Are”**

We take the first steps in learning about the Unfiltered life with God. Being Unfiltered with God means coming before him exactly as we are, without pretending or hiding our questions, doubts, failures, and struggles. Through the life of David, a shepherd who became king and faced many challenges, we learn what it means to bring our true selves before God. We begin to explore David’s prayer in Psalm 139, where he asks God to search his heart. We often focus on what is happening around us without taking the time to examine what is happening within us, but when we invite God to search our hearts, we allow him to reveal what is shaping us and begin to understand who we really are in him.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139, Ezekiel 36:26

### MONDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Truth: Know Me”**

Jesus knows us completely, including the worries, pressures, and uncertainties we carry. We often allow the expectations of others, fear of the future, and our desire for control to take priority in our lives. However, Jesus reminds us that we do not have to carry these burdens alone. When we seek him first and trust in his plan, we can begin to let go of what is weighing us down and find peace in knowing that he loves us and walks with us.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:5–10, Matthew 6:25–34

### TUESDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Grace: See Me”**

We often try to hide the parts of ourselves that we do not want others to see. Our mistakes, failures, and sins can make us feel unworthy of love and forgiveness. However, God sees us completely and loves us despite our imperfections. Through Jesus, we are offered grace, forgiveness, and new life. We do not have to hide from God because his grace is greater than our failures. When we come before him as we truly are, we can receive his grace and begin a new life in Christ.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:11–16, John 3:16, Romans 5:20, Ephesians 2:4–5, 2 Corinthians 12:9

### WEDNESDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Path: Lead Me”**

Jesus knows who we are, where we have been, and what lies ahead of us. Even when we cannot see the path before us or understand his plan, we can trust him to lead us. Following Jesus requires us to make the active decision to surrender control and allow him to guide our lives. When we reflect on what has been shaping our decisions and the direction of our lives, we can begin to take steps toward the life God is calling us to live.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:17–18, 23–24, 2 Peter 1:3–11

### THURSDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Life”**

Living an Unfiltered life with God is not something that ends when we leave camp. Throughout the week, we have learned what it means to invite God to search our hearts, know us completely, see us as we truly are, and lead us in our everyday lives. As we return home, we are challenged to continue living authentically with God, trusting him through our struggles, decisions, and opportunities. An Unfiltered life is one where we continue to seek Jesus and allow him to shape who we are and how we live.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:1–3, 7–10, 23–24
