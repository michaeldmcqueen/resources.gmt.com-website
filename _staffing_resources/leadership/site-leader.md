The site leader supports youth leaders and the Red Shirt staff at one Week of Hope location for the whole summer. The site leader checks in with youth leaders during each camp week, builds relationships with them, helps resolve issues, and gives the summer staff encouragement and helpful feedback. The site leader reports to the Week of Hope Project Manager and, on site, works most closely with the Project Leader. The responsibilities of the site leader include, but are not limited to, the following:

###### Minimum Job Requirements

* Be at least 25 years old
* Live within driving distance of the Week of Hope location and provide your own transportation to and from the lodging facility
* Be able to work legally in the US
* Pass a national criminal background check and provide two references, preferably from organizations where you have worked with young people
* Complete all site leader training (by phone, video call, or online) and mandated reporter training before your first camp week
* Be reachable by phone during camp weeks for youth leaders and summer staff
* Work closely and effectively with the Red Shirt staff by supporting and encouraging them to ensure an excellent camp experience for each participant
* Invest in youth leaders and youth
* Ability to accept supervision and constructive criticism
* Wear the red staff shirt and a GMT name tag while on site, and follow the GMT dress code

###### Position Job Requirements

* Arrive on Sunday around 3:00 PM to welcome youth leaders at check-in, and introduce yourself at the 4:30 PM adult meeting
* On Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday, spend about two hours at the lodging facility between 4:00 and 7:00 PM
* On those days, check in person with the primary youth leader from each youth group to listen for issues, encourage and pray with them, and answer questions
* Check in with the summer staff each camp day, with the Project Leader as your main contact. Some check-ins can be by phone or text when that fits the week
* Share any issues youth leaders raise with the summer staff, and work as a team to resolve them swiftly and effectively, referring to GMT training
* During each visit, walk through the facility (kitchen, showers, sleeping rooms, care card rack, and signs) and be available to help in other areas of camp if needed
* Complete a weekly evaluation of camp with the summer staff on Thursday and make adjustments to improve the camp experience
* Write a thank-you note to each primary youth leader after each camp week
* Complete the end-of-camp survey each week, and log your hours and submit them to the Staffing Team before you leave on Thursday
* If you cannot be at camp one week, tell the Week of Hope Project Manager ahead of time and arrange with the summer staff to check in with youth leaders in your place
* At the end of the summer, complete a Week of Hope Leadership Evaluation for each summer staff member

###### Physical Requirements

* Able to walk the lodging facility and stand for 2 hours at a time
* Able to lift objects up to 15 pounds
* Comfortable spending busy, high-energy camp evenings with up to 100 participants

###### Compensation

This is a paid, part-time seasonal position at $16.00 per hour, up to 15 hours per week (as much as $240 per week). Site leaders serve for six or seven camp weeks, from June 20th through August 5th, 2027. Hours are logged each Thursday and paid biweekly. Training time is paid hourly as well.
