We suggest you have this meeting within a few weeks of coming home, while the stories are still fresh.

Your church prayed for your team, gave to your trip, and sent you out at the [Commissioning Service](/workcamps/3-pretrip-meetings/commissioning-service/). Now it's time to celebrate, tell them what God did, and thank them.

### **Before the Meeting**

* Pick a date and invite families, supporters, and the whole congregation.
* Gather photos and short videos from the week, and put together a slideshow.
* Ask three or four students and one adult to each share a short story (2 to 3 minutes).
* Have everyone wear their camp T-shirt.
* Print a [Community Service certificate](/workcamps/6-post-camp-resources/community-service-certificate-2023/) for each participant (under Post Camp Resources).
* **Optional:** Serve snacks or a meal. It makes it feel like a party!

### **Welcome**

**Say:** Thank you all for coming! A few weeks ago, you sent us out to serve in **(your mission trip location)**. You prayed for us every day, and many of you gave so we could go. Tonight we want to show you what that looked like.

**(Play the slideshow.)**

### **Stories From the Week**

Invite your students and adult to share. These questions can help them get ready:

* Who is one person you met that you'll never forget?
* What was the hardest part of the week, and what got you through it?
* Where did you see God at work?
* What did you learn about yourself?
* What will you do differently now that you're home?

### **Thank Your Supporters**

* Thank everyone who prayed, gave, or helped with fundraising and the Community Gift.
* Have students write thank-you notes to the people who supported them, like the people who got their [Support Letter](/workcamps/5-helpful-resources/support-letter/).
* Hand out the Community Service certificates.

### **Sharing With the Church**

If you can, ask for a few minutes during a Sunday service as well:

* Show a few photos.
* Have one or two students share a short story.
* Thank the congregation for their prayers and support.

### **Keep It Going**

**Say:** Serving doesn't have to stop because the trip is over. There are people right here who need help too.

* Talk about one way your group could serve in your own community this year.
* Do the [Post-camp Devotion](/workcamps/6-post-camp-resources/post-camp-devotion-precamp/) together.
* Ask leaders and participants to fill out the [Workcamp Survey](/workcamps/6-post-camp-resources/workcamp-survey-2/).
* Share the dates for next summer's trip, and invite new students to come along!

### **Close in Prayer**

"God, thank you for keeping our team safe and for everything you did this week. Thank you for the people we served and for everyone who sent us. Help us keep shining your light right here at home. In Jesus' name, amen."
