Group Mission Trips does its best to accommodate every participant’s needs, and we take dietary restrictions and allergies very seriously.

### 🟢 Can Accommodate

We can accommodate these dietary restrictions:

* Peanut allergy (not airborne). Peanut butter is on our menu.
* Nut allergy (not airborne)
* Egg allergy
* Seed allergy
* Soy allergy

*Our kitchens are not gluten-free, nut-free, or dairy-free.*

### 🟡 Can Partially Accommodate

Participants with these dietary restrictions will need to bring some of their own food:

* Gluten allergy. Let us know, and we can have a few gluten-free items available.
* Two or more allergies. Combinations of allergies and preferences are often hard to accommodate.
* Lactose intolerance
* Vegetarian or vegan diet. We offer a full salad bar, but you may need to bring some protein options.
* Picky eaters

### 🔴 Cannot Accommodate

Participants with these dietary restrictions need to bring their own food and attend at their own risk:

* Celiac disease. We cannot guarantee there is no cross-contamination.
* Airborne nut allergy. We cannot guarantee that our lodging facility or work sites will be nut-free.
* Any allergy so severe that a trace amount would cause a reaction

Group Mission Trips cannot discount participant fees when someone needs to bring their own food.

**Please put all dietary restrictions and severe allergies in the Special Requests section of your customer account, so we can let the camp staff know ahead of time.**
