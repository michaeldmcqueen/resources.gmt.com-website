## Theme: Unfiltered

**Theme Verse: “Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. See if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me along the way everlasting.” – Psalm 139:23–24**

### SUNDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered: Come as You Really Are”**

Through the Absolutely Nothing Challenge and a teenager’s look at Psalm 139, campers discover what it means to be real with God and let him meet them where they are. Then they meet their crews, choose their roles, and dedicate their crews to God.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139

### MONDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Heart: Search Me”**

Campers move from searching for hidden objects to letting Jesus search their hearts. Through a crew scavenger hunt, a spontaneous drama, and personal reflection, they explore the filters they may carry and discover that Jesus already knows, loves, and wants to transform the real them. The night ends with Care Cards, encouraging campers to see the good in others.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:1–4, Ezekiel 36:26

### TUESDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Truth: Know Me”**

Jesus knows campers completely, so they don’t have to hide what they’re carrying. They’ll explore worry, pressure, and the call to seek Jesus first, and learn to trust the One who knows them, loves them, and invites them to follow him.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:5–10, Matthew 6:25–34

### WEDNESDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Grace: See Me”**

We often hide the parts of ourselves we don’t want others to see. Tonight, through a dramatic story, campers discover that God sees them completely, loves them anyway, and through Jesus offers grace, forgiveness, and new life. They’re invited to stop hiding, receive his grace, and trust Jesus.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:11–16, John 3:16, Romans 5:20, Ephesians 2:4–5, 2 Corinthians 12:9

### THURSDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Path: Lead Me”**

Because Jesus knows the real us, sees the real us, and loves the real us, we can trust him to lead us. Campers think about what has been shaping their path and respond at four interactive stations. Then they’re invited to the cross to pray, surrender what they’ve been carrying, and ask Jesus to lead them one step at a time.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:17–18, 23–24, 2 Peter 1:3–11

### FRIDAY PROGRAM

**“Unfiltered Life”**

A celebration of the week and a bridge to life back home. Campers look back at the Unfiltered journey (Search Me, Know Me, See Me, Lead Me) and spend time with their crew and resident in One More Circle. The night turns toward home with a commissioning and the 2028 theme reveal.

**Scripture:** Psalm 139:1–3, 7–10, 23–24
